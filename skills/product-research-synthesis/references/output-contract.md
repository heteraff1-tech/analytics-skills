# Final output contract

Write a layered Markdown report.

## 1. Executive conclusion

In 3–7 paragraphs:

- what is happening;
- why it matters;
- the strongest evidence;
- the main uncertainty;
- the decision or next move.

## 2. Decision, scope, and evidence quality

State the decision, materials analyzed, periods/segments covered, excluded
materials, and an evidence-quality verdict. Surface any issue that can invalidate
the rest of the report.

## 3. Current product picture

Describe the product state across the relevant lifecycle: acquisition,
activation, engagement, retention, monetization, and user needs. Include only
dimensions supported by evidence.

## 4. Key findings

For each `F-*` finding:

- claim;
- quantitative evidence IDs;
- qualitative evidence IDs;
- counterevidence;
- relationship type;
- confidence;
- product implication;
- affected segments;
- open question.

## 5. Hypothesis ledger

Use a table:

| ID | Original hypothesis | Status | Evidence for | Evidence against | What changed | Next test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Do not hide contradicted or untested hypotheses.

## 6. What changed

Use:

| We believed | New evidence | We now believe | Confidence | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Separate genuine change from merely improved wording.

## 7. Goals

Use:

| Goal | Previous definition | Status | Recommended definition | Evidence/rationale | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Do not invent a numeric target when no baseline or business constraint supports
one.

## 8. Contradictions and segment differences

Explain disagreements rather than averaging them. State whether each is
measurement, segment, timing, selection, causality, or unresolved.

## 9. Gaps

Use:

| Gap | Category | Decision impact | Priority | Smallest closing action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Include unknown unknowns suggested by missing segments, missing events, or
one-sided research.

## 10. New hypotheses and opportunities

For each new hypothesis provide mechanism, expected observable signal,
counter-signal, and smallest test. Keep opportunities distinct from solution
commitments.

## 11. Recommended next steps

Rank actions. Include expected learning and stop/success criteria. Distinguish
decisions possible now from work needed to reduce uncertainty.

## 12. Limitations and appendix

Append:

- evidence ledger/index;
- participant coverage;
- metric definitions;
- calculation outputs;
- secondary patterns;
- complete hypothesis and gap registers;
- source files and locators.

## Style

- Verdict before supporting detail.
- Clear prose, not a quote wall or dashboard dump.
- Exact numbers with denominators.
- Exact, short quotes only where they add meaning.
- Explicit confidence and caveats.
- No causal wording beyond the evidence.
- No generic recommendation such as “improve onboarding” without the specific
  behavior, segment, mechanism, and test.
