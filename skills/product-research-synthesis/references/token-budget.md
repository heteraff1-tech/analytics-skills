# Token and context budget

The goal is a broad result, not a broad active context.

## Depth profiles

| Profile | Core report | Evidence appendix | Use |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `compact` | 1,200–2,000 words | minimal | one decision or small dataset |
| `standard` | 2,500–4,500 words | targeted | default full synthesis |
| `deep` | 4,500–8,000 words | extensive | broad portfolio/research review |

The limits are targets, not reasons to omit a material contradiction or safety
caveat. Put supporting detail in appendices rather than repeating it.

## Progressive disclosure

- Keep this orchestrator and its local references active.
- Load at most one upstream base method and one specialist per normal phase.
- Causal analysis may load the three causal files.
- Experiment analysis may load the GrowthBook router, one lifecycle reference,
  and one platform-neutral reader only when necessary.
- Do not load all five upstream repositories or all selected skills together.

## Raw source batching

### Interviews and long text

- Inventory all artifacts first.
- Process batches of 4–6 interviews or approximately 15k–20k input tokens,
  whichever comes first.
- Convert each interview into an evidence card of at most 120 words plus exact
  locators and up to two short quotes.
- After a batch, retain evidence cards and discard the raw transcript from the
  active working context.
- Cluster only after artifact-level coding is complete.
- Keep no more than 8 principal patterns in the report body; put valid secondary
  patterns in the appendix.

### Quantitative data

- Never paste a full raw table into the reasoning or report.
- Use code/query tools for aggregation.
- Keep only definitions, quality checks, and result tables needed for the
  decision.
- Limit a displayed table to 25 rows unless the extra rows are themselves the
  point.
- For segmentation, start with pre-specified high-value segments; do not fish
  across every dimension.
- Preserve generated outputs as files and reference them by name.

## Checkpoint compression

At the end of every phase, write the named compact artifact. Later phases read
that artifact, not all preceding raw inputs. Each finding should reuse evidence
IDs rather than restating the underlying quote or calculation.

Suggested active limits for a standard run:

- up to 80 atomic evidence records in the working ledger;
- up to 20 live hypotheses;
- up to 12 synthesized findings;
- up to 15 prioritized gaps/actions.

Additional valid material goes to an appendix file.

## Output economy

- Lead with the conclusion and decision implications.
- Use one or two representative quotes per major qualitative finding.
- Do not repeat the same number in executive summary, findings, hypotheses, and
  next steps unless the repetition changes its role.
- Separate the report body from evidence tables and calculation outputs.
- When evidence is insufficient, say so once clearly and specify the missing
  test; do not compensate with speculative prose.
